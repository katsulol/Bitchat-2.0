## Task 6 Closure Notes
- Caught and resolved two real runtime/compliance gaps during Task 6 execution:
  1. `BitchatCoreService::send_message_with_deadline` (`core/src/api.rs`) now strictly enforces `VolatileSessionState` (`PendingNegotiation` and `UnsupportedByPeer` fail closed without SQLite fallback; `Active` forces `MessageMode::Privacy` and clamps deadlines to negotiated retention).
  2. `BitchatCoreService::poll_inbound` (`core/src/api.rs`) increased its bounded `node.poll_step` drain cap from `10` to `64` steps so a full 512-byte envelope fragmented at minimum 23-byte BLE ATT MTU (~28 fragments) reassembles cleanly within a single poll tick.
  3. `Session::encrypt` (`core/src/crypto.rs`) now rejects `*send_nonce == u64::MAX` before calling `transport.write_message`, honoring the Noise reserved sentinel nonce `2^64 - 1`.
